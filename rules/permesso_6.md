# Permesso for six (Permesso)

6 players. In the book of 1787: pp. 76-77. Status: playable, with decisions of ours where the book is silent. Rules revision 11.

This game is written as [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md) with differences.

Sestilio, Permesso for six (Torino 1787, «Del giuoco di Permesso in sei persone, o sia Sestilio», pp. 76-77), a game the book calls «inusitato»: 13 cards to each of the six players, the dealer too, and no discard (p. 76); whoever wants to go alone asks «Permesso» as before. The book offers «giuoco sciolto» instead, played by the rules of Dottore without a discard, where the most points wins (pp. 76-77); here it is the variant «sciolto_chosen» (nobody bids: the cards are dealt and play begins at once, each for himself), and the outcome of a general pass in Dummett (1980, p. 267) is the variant «all_pass_sciolto». The cards are counted one by one, two by two or three by three as agreed (p. 75). The payments of both are not printed. Marks: [D] documented, [R] our reading, [S] sources silent.

## How to read the marks

Each rule is marked by where it comes from. **Documented** [D]: printed in the named source, with its pages. **Our reading** [R]: the source is ambiguous or the sources disagree; the other reading is shown under the rule.

**Sources silent** [S]: the sources say nothing, so the decision is ours, made so that the game can be played. Where the question is open, the rule names the open question.

### Who plays

- Play passes to the right. *Documented: from the book, p. 35.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: seats.direction -->

### The cards

- The whole pack of 78 is used: 22 trumps (the Fool is among them as number 0) and 14 cards in each of four suits. *Our reading: book, p. 76.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.deck -->
- Trumps rank by their numbers, but the Angel (XX) takes the World (XXI). *Documented: from the book, pp. 9-10.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.trump_order -->
- In swords and batons the larger number is higher; in cups and coins it is the other way: the ace takes the two, up to the ten, and the king takes the queen, the knight and the jack. *Documented: from the book, pp. 11-13.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.suit_order -->
- Kings, the Angel, the Bagatto and the Fool are worth 5, queens 4, knights 3, jacks 2, every other card 1. *Documented: from the book, pp. 13-14.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.card_points -->
- The Fool takes nothing and is taken by nothing: it is shown in place of a card. *Documented: from the book, p. 77.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.fool -->
- Whoever plays the Fool keeps it and gives another card in its place. *Our reading: book, pp. 77-78.* Other reading: Nothing is given for the Fool. Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.fool_exchange -->
- The owner of the Fool picks the card he gives. *Our reading: book, p. 52.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.fool_exchange_card -->
- The card for the Fool goes to the winner of the trick. *Documented: from the book, pp. 77-78.* Same as in [Permesso for five](permesso_5.md). <!-- rule: cards.fool_exchange_to -->

### By agreement / Variants

- «two cards»: rank 2, exchange 2, Onoranza 3 soldi, sides: the declarer against the rest. *Documented: from the book, p. 62.* <!-- rule: contracts.two -->

### Offences

- The offence «calling for cards without laying a discard» cannot happen at this table. By the book, the offender pays the Onoranza to each of the others; then the hand is played out. *Documented: from the book, pp. 64-65.* <!-- rule: penalties.call_without_laying -->
- The offence «refusing the bid one made» cannot happen at this table. By the book, the offender pays a double Onoranza to each of the others; with a marcio, a game with a marcia. *Documented: from the book, p. 66.* <!-- rule: penalties.refuse_bid -->
- This table does not charge the offence «a signal to the partner»: the penalty stands in the text of the rules only. By the book, the offender pays the simple stake to each of the others; then the lone player may stop. *Documented: from the book, p. 73.* <!-- rule: penalties.signal -->
- The offence «revoke» cannot happen at this table. By the book, the offender pays the simple stake to each of the others. *Documented: from the book, pp. 33-34.* <!-- rule: penalties.revoke -->
